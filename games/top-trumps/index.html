Ferry Top Trumps

36 West Coast ferries, one AI skipper. Pick the stat you think beats the other card. Win both cards, take all 36 to win the game.

How to play

  1. Both players draw their top card. Only you can see yours.
  2. Whoever's turn it is picks a stat. Bigger number wins, except Draught, where the shallower ship wins (it can reach more harbours), and Year, where the newer ship wins.
  3. The winner takes both cards and picks next. A draw puts both cards in the middle for the next winner.
  4. The Captain plays the stat its card is strongest at. The Deckhand just guesses.
