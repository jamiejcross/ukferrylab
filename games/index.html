Games

Beat the computer with ferry cards, pack a car deck without tipping the ship, or captain five crossings on as little fuel as you can.

  • Top Trumps36 West Coast ferries against a computer skipper. Pick the number you think will win.
  • Cargo GameLoad lorries and cars onto the car deck. Keep her level and above the Plimsoll mark, then ask to sail.
  • Speed vs FuelChoose your speed for five crossings. Arrive on time, burn as little fuel as you can, and get your sums right.
Ship figures are rounded and simplified for learning.
